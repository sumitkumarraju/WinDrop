Looking for your PC

Keep your iPhone on the same Wi-Fi as your PC.

Photos, videos, ZIP, RAR — any file, any size. Choose Choose Files in the menu to send originals.

Tip: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. WinDrop will connect by itself every time you open it.
